# Rights and provenance

## Creator's public statement

In the [10 July 2024 interview with The Hardboiled GMshoe's Office](https://gmshoe.wordpress.com/2024/07/10/qa-tate-renforth-strange-heroes/), Tate RF (Tate Renforth) said at 9:27 PM:

> Strange Heroes is completely open source.

The surrounding answer explains an intention to let community creators legally publish their work. We acknowledge that affirmative statement rather than treating the project as closed. The [official Monken8 playtest page](https://monken8.itch.io/strange-heroes-playtest) provides the v1.02 playtest as a name-your-own-price download. Both sources were checked on 26 October 2026.

The inspected pages do not specify an OSI/Creative Commons license identifier or separate terms for every included artwork. This toolkit therefore includes **original restatements of game procedures and factual mechanics**, plus citations, not a mirror of the PDF or its artwork, layout, character sheets or full prose talent catalog. It does not claim that the creator selected MIT for those materials. If a formal license is supplied later, record its exact scope before expanding redistribution.

## What is licensed here

The repository's original Python code, original instructions, original rules summaries and fictional examples are MIT licensed by Jet Black RLSH. That license grants rights only in this project's original contributions; it does not claim ownership of Strange Heroes or change third-party rights. Strange Heroes terminology is used to identify interoperability and inspiration. No endorsement is implied.

QuestForge v1.3.0 (MIT, Adrián Melic) was reviewed as inspiration for skill-led GM tooling. No implementation code or its D&D primer is copied. See `NOTICE` for attribution.

## Source baseline

- Strange Heroes Playtest v1.02, 94 PDF pages, by Tate RF / Monken8; official download above.
- Selected printed-page references are in `rules-sources.md` and the original `quick-reference.md`.
- The interview's 9:00 PM answer explicitly describes success on equal-or-higher TC. That supports the `success` tie policy, while v1.02 p7's wording and p29's defence wording are recorded transparently. The CLI requires a declared policy rather than silently treating an interpretation as unambiguous printed canon.
- Local imports fingerprint the exact user-supplied file with SHA-256. Full text/index stays private and is not distributed in this source release.
