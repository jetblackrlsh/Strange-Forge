# Strange Heroes lore-card PDF

The [complete lore-card PDF](Strange-Heroes-Lore-Cards.pdf) contains **27 distinct identities and 54 pages**, alternating each card's front and back. Each page is **5×7 inches** (360×504 PDF points): an oversized digital reading proof, not standard pocket cards or commercial duplex imposition. Bleed, cutting guides and duplex registration are not supplied.

## Original contribution and sources

This is an unofficial companion to **Tate RF / Monken8's Strange Heroes Playtest v1.02** and the official **Pregenerated Characters**, **The Zoo Rescue** and **Concert Killer** supplements, available from the [creator's playtest page](https://monken8.itch.io/strange-heroes-playtest). The broader [lore guide](lore-guide.md) identifies these publications and their setting context. Individual cards retain source references.

The deck combines newly AI-generated portraits with original lore paraphrases, published numeric fields and complete printed power-name/rank lists. It does not mirror the official PDF, reproduce its illustrations, or supply the full prose of the rulebook. Characters and underlying source material remain attributable to their respective creators. No creator endorsement is implied; see [rights and provenance](rights-and-provenance.md).

## What is and is not established

- Twenty unique published stat-block identities are represented, with Ian Ciraulo explicitly using the source-specified Manticore Agent profile rather than an invented build.
- Six supporting figures lack dedicated published numerical profiles: **Brian Byrne, Gregory Beak, Richard-Quinn Larenzo, Billy Page, Abe Harrison and Lenny**. The cards disclose those omissions. Gregory's narrative abilities do not receive an invented rank; Lenny does not inherit Gold Star's statistics.
- Repeated Black Nimbus sheets are deduplicated. Generic profiles remain labelled archetypes. Printed anomalies and equipment dependencies are retained rather than silently recalculated. Health and Energy damage resistance remain distinct; printed frustration capacity is not an initial award.
- Portraits are original visual interpretations. Redacted or unspecified costumes are not newly discovered canon. Thor's fully clothed green outfit is an artistic interpretation; the pregen illustration is bare-chested. Magnos's generated healed-arm markings are interpretive; the source specifically mentions his left arm.
- The PDF is a reading export, not an editable vector source. Editable SVGs and separate portraits are not part of this repository publication.

The completed deck was checked against the audited source transcription for the twenty published profiles, Ian's shared profile, all **157 power entries/categories**, and retained lore/features/notes. All 54 source faces were rendered and inspected before this PDF was produced. This is a manual/source-and-layout QA claim, not a claim that Strange Forge automatically validates every game rule.
