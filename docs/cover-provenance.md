# Cover provenance

`assets/strange-forge-cover.png` is an original AI-generated modern-anime composition created for Strange Forge on 27 October 2026. It is a new rendering, not a scan, crop or repainting of official artwork.

The featured **Strange Heroes** pregenerated heroes are, left to right:

- **Micro-Man / Ted Wood** — shrinking hero.
- **Thor** — mythic thunder hero.
- **Captain Midnight / Alex Archer** — costumed hero.
- **Red Orchid / Josefina Santiago** — psychic hero.

Character references were checked against the creator's official **Pregenerated Characters** PDF, pages **1, 3, 5 and 7**, available from the [Strange Heroes playtest download page](https://monken8.itch.io/strange-heroes-playtest) by Tate RF / Monken8. The composition interprets those references; it is not a claim that this exact team pose, city, scene or visual style is official canon.

Strange Heroes and its characters remain associated with their creator. This fan-made toolkit and cover are unofficial and not endorsed by Tate RF / Monken8. See [rights and provenance](rights-and-provenance.md) for the creator's open-source statement and the scope of this project's original contributions. No official book, character-sheet artwork or full text is bundled here.
